Method

Exposure % is annual OpenAI-linked revenue divided by trailing-twelve-month revenue. Where OpenAI revenue is disclosed (Microsoft) it is used directly. Where only a multi-year contract is public, the contract value is spread evenly across its term and marked est. Second-order paths (Arista via Microsoft, Dell via CoreWeave) multiply a disclosed customer share by that customer's OpenAI share, also marked est.

$ at risk = exposure % × TTM revenue, recomputed whenever market data updates. It measures revenue tied to OpenAI, not a forecast of losses. Exposure inputs were checked against filings and press on 2026-10-12. Market data refreshes after each US close: prices from Yahoo Finance, market cap from price × share count, TTM revenue from SEC XBRL filings.

Screened out: no citable OpenAI link

    Sources are SEC filings, company releases and earnings calls, and CNBC/Bloomberg/WSJ reporting, linked in each company's panel. Not investment advice.
